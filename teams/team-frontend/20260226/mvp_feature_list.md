# Freeweight MVP Feature List

1. **Coach program builder** — coaches can create workouts with exercises, sets, reps, and percentage of max, and assign them to athletes or groups on a schedule

2. **Athlete workout execution** — athletes can open today's workout, see auto-calculated weights based on their recorded maxes, and log sets with weight, reps, and RPE

3. **Coach-athlete connection** — coaches generate an invite code, athletes enter it to join a roster and receive assigned programming

4. **Coach monitoring dashboard** — coaches can see who completed today's workout, view individual logs, and receive injury flags from athletes

5. **Spreadsheet import** — coaches can upload an existing spreadsheet and have it converted via AI into a structured Freeweight program

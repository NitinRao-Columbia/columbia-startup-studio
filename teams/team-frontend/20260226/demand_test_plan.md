# Freeweight Demand Test Plan

## Audience

Collegiate strength and conditioning coaches, starting with Columbia University and expanding to peer institutions (Ivy League, NESCAC, similar D1/D3 programs). Reached via direct outreach through email, LinkedIn, and warm introductions from existing Columbia coach relationships.

---

## Message Angles

**Angle A: Pain (the spreadsheet)**
Lead with the problem. Coaches are managing athlete programs through spreadsheets and group texts. Freeweight replaces that with a tool built specifically for team strength programming.

> *"Still running your program out of a spreadsheet? Freeweight gives you team programming, auto-calculated weights, and real-time completion tracking — built for S&C coaches."*

**Angle B: Proof (Columbia coaches use it)**
Lead with social proof and specificity. Columbia S&C coaches are already on it. Positioned as a tool built by athletes, validated by coaches at a real program.

> *"Columbia's strength coaches use Freeweight to program for their teams. Auto-calculated weights, workout tracking, and full roster visibility — no spreadsheets."*

---

## Budget

$0. All demand testing is done through direct outreach. No paid media.

Channels:
- Direct email to S&C coaches at target schools
- LinkedIn DMs
- Warm intros from Columbia coaches already on the platform
- Jackie's existing coach network

---

## Success Threshold

- **Primary metric:** Signups from outreach
- **Success threshold:** 5 teams signed up within 2 weeks of outreach start
- **Secondary metric:** Response rate above 20% indicates message resonance even if signups lag
- Angle that gets more responses in the first week gets used for the remainder of outreach

---

## Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Conversion works end-to-end (thank-you page fires after signup)
- [ ] UTMs on every outreach link to track which angle and which contact drove signups
- [ ] Message templates written and reviewed for both angles
- [ ] Landing page loads correctly on mobile
- [ ] Signup form connected to a tracked destination (email list or Airtable)
- [ ] Contact list compiled and deduplicated before outreach begins
- [ ] Follow-up sequence planned (initial message + one follow-up after 5 days)

# weekly_update_2026-04-21.md

## What we built last week
Athlete workout execution is live. Athletes can open today's assigned workout, see their sets with weights already calculated from their maxes, and log each set with weight, reps, and RPE. We also shipped drag and drop reordering of exercises — athletes can rearrange the order they complete exercises within a session. This came up in user testing and coach conversations. Athletes frequently want to warm up differently or work around equipment availability in a busy gym, and forcing a fixed order was creating friction. Coaches are fine with it because the logged data still captures everything.

## What we're building this week
Workout summaries for both coaches and athletes. Coaches have asked to see a recap after each session — who did what, what weights were used, any notes or flags. Athletes have asked for the same on their end. This closes the feedback loop that makes the product sticky for both sides.

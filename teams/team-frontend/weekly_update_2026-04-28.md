# weekly_update_2026-04-28.md

## What we built last week
Post-workout summaries are live for both sides. After a session is completed, athletes see a recap of what they did — exercises, sets, weights, RPE. Coaches see the same from their dashboard, per athlete, with any modifications or injury flags highlighted. This was the most requested feature from coaches we have onboarded so far. Several of them were texting athletes after sessions to ask how it went. The summary replaces that entirely.

## What we're building this week
Spreadsheet import with AI conversion. Every coach we have talked to has an existing program in Google Sheets or Excel. Rebuilding it from scratch in Freeweight is the biggest onboarding friction point. We are building a flow where coaches upload their spreadsheet and our AI reads it and converts it into a structured Freeweight program automatically. If this works, onboarding time for a new coach drops from an hour to a few minutes.

## Biggest blocker
Spreadsheet formats vary wildly between coaches. Building the AI conversion to handle enough variation to be useful without requiring a perfectly formatted input is the main engineering challenge this week.
